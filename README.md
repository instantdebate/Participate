# Participate

I made this app for my best friend, Ryan.

**Participate** asks one simple question:

> Did I do something today that made me a participant in my life instead of merely an observer?

## How it works

A participation moment can be **Create, Move, Connect, Explore, or Improve**. One moment is enough to earn the day a star.

There are intentionally no streak resets, red missed days, negative points, or leaderboards.

The optional “felt alive” rating and weekly reflection are there to help notice which activities actually make life feel fuller.

## Notify Bryan

The app can open an email to `bryanpharris@gmail.com` containing the latest participation moment. The user reviews and sends the message.

## Privacy

Entries and reflections are stored locally in the browser on the user’s device. They are not uploaded to this repository.

The friendship photo and Bryan’s notification email address are part of the published app source.
